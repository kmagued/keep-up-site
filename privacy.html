Keep Up

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

In short

Keep Up has no accounts and no servers. The people you keep up with, your calls, reminders and everything else you add stay on your phone. Nothing about you is collected, sold or shared.

Who makes Keep Up

Keep Up is made by Khaled Magued, an independent developer. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean the developer, and “Keep Up” means the app on iPhone and Android.

What Keep Up uses on your phone

  • Contacts. With your permission, Keep Up reads your contacts so you can choose who to keep up with, and to pick up new names, numbers and birthdays. It keeps only the people you choose, in its own storage on the phone. Your contacts are never sent anywhere.
  • Calls. When you call someone from Keep Up, it notes on the phone whether the call connected and when it ended, so it can mark that you talked. It never sees or records what was said. On Android, if you turn on “Use call log”, Keep Up reads the log on the phone to mark calls you made; nothing from it leaves the phone.
  • Notifications. Your morning and evening reminders are scheduled on the phone. No push service is used.

You can turn any of these permissions off in your phone’s Settings at any time.

Where your information is kept

Everything you add is kept in Keep Up’s storage on your phone. It’s included in your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) if you have that turned on, under Apple’s or Google’s terms. Deleting the app deletes everything in it, except backups you saved yourself.

When Keep Up goes online

  • App updates. Keep Up checks for small updates through Expo’s update service. The check sends the app’s version, your phone’s platform, and a random install number used to deliver updates. It contains nothing about you, your contacts or what you add.
  • Keep Up Plus. Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play, under their own privacy policies. Keep Up never sees your payment details. It asks the store, on your phone, whether you have Plus.

That’s all. Keep Up has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services that collect information about you.

Backups you make

With Plus, you can save a backup file. It goes only where you choose to save it, like Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive, and is never sent to me.

Children

Keep Up doesn’t collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
