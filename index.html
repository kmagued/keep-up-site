Keep Up

Call the people who matter, before too long goes by.

Keep Up tells you who to call today, remembers what to ask them, and keeps every birthday in view. Everything stays on your phone.

Help

Where is my information kept?

On your phone, in Keep Up’s own storage. There are no accounts and no servers. Your phone’s backup (iCloud or Google) includes it if you have that turned on. With Plus you can also save a backup file anywhere you like.

I got a new phone. How do I bring Plus back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open Keep Up, and tap Keep Up Plus › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel Plus?

On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Keep Up Plus. On Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Calls aren’t being marked as talked.

Start the call from Keep Up, so it can notice when the call connects. If it can’t tell, it asks “Did you reach them?” when you come back. On Android, you can also turn on “Use call log” in Settings.

I’m not getting my morning reminder.

Check that notifications are allowed for Keep Up in your phone’s Settings, and that the time in Keep Up › Settings › Morning reminder is right.
